Audio Finder

Before you enable the connector

Audio Finder Browser Connector reads the titles, tab and window identifiers, and mute state of non-incognito tabs that Chrome or Brave marks as audible.

It sends that information only to the Audio Finder app on this Mac at 127.0.0.1. It does not read or send URLs, browsing history, incognito tabs, page content beyond the audible tab title, or audio. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

The production Mac app uses fixed local ports 17654 and 17655. You can disable the connector at any time by clearing the checkbox above and choosing Save.
